4.
### **Question:**

According to the maintenance proposal for the Ammonia Feed Pump, what is the recommended repair action for the shaft (Item No. 5) if its measured runout is 0.15mm or greater during a major inspection?

### **Answer:**

If the measured runout of the shaft is 0.15mm or over, the recommended action is to **replace the shaft with a new item**. For a runout less than 0.15mm, a repair is permissible.

**Source:** Page 2 of the document (PDF page 2), in the table for "Recommended Inspection Interval: 4-6 years (Major Inspection)", under Item No. 5, in the "Repair" column. - Ammonia Maintenance Schedule.pdf
